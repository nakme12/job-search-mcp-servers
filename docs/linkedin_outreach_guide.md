## 2️⃣ Manual Connection Outreach (5‑10 contacts)
1. Open the tabs created by `open_linkedin_tasks.cjs` (already opened).  
2. Pick 5‑10 contacts from the table above.  
3. For each profile: click **Connect**, skip the note if you prefer, then **Send**.  
4. Close the tab or keep it open for later follow‑up.  
5. Record the names you contacted in a simple text file to avoid duplicates.

## 4️⃣ Founder / Company Follow
Search each company name on LinkedIn, open its **Company Page**, and click **Follow**. Also follow any senior founder/lead you can locate on the page (often a **Follow** button appears next to their name).

## 5️⃣ Daily Engagement Routine (30‑60 min)
1. Open the two saved‑search URLs (already opened):
   - https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/content/?datePosted=%22past-24h%22&keywords=hiring%20react%20indore&origin=FACETED_SEARCH&sortBy=%22date_posted%22
   - https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/content/?datePosted=%22past-24h%22&keywords=hiring%20react%20remote%20india&origin=FACETED_SEARCH&sortBy=%22date_posted%22
2. Like every relevant post you see.  
3. Leave a brief comment on 3‑5 posts (e.g., “Great opportunity, thanks for sharing!”).  
4. Save any posts you plan to apply to (click the **Save** icon).  
5. Repeat daily – the algorithm will start surfacing more front‑end hiring posts.
